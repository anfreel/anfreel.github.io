My research examines how artificial intelligence influences human judgment and decision-making. I am particularly interested in how the design and presentation of AI-generated advice shape people's decisions, trust, and autonomy.

## Human–AI Decision-Making

I investigate how people evaluate and respond to AI-generated recommendations, particularly when those recommendations conflict with their initial judgments.

My work examines how factors such as advice timing, perceived severity, and social consensus influence people's decisions.

## Persuasion and Social Influence in AI

I am interested in how conversational AI systems use persuasive communication and how different forms of persuasion affect human decision-making.

This research direction explores the relationship between rational argumentation, manipulative communication, and human autonomy.

## AI Governance and Accountability

My interdisciplinary research interests extend to the legal and policy challenges of artificial intelligence.

I am particularly interested in questions of responsibility, accountability, and the governance of AI systems across complex technological environments.
